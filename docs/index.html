Download & keep

Save now.
Watch later.

Completed downloads play after an app restart without internet or a fresh provider login. Choose private app storage or a local folder on supported device, SD or USB storage.

Direct movie and episode files are supported. Adaptive-stream downloads, offline DRM licensing and live recording remain planned. Read the offline viewing guide for details.

  1. Choose a title

    Open a supported movie or episode and tap Download for offline viewing. Unmetered connections are used by default.

  2. Manage Downloads

    See progress and file sizes, pause or retry transfers, and play completed titles. Settings → Downloads chooses where new files are saved.

  3. Keep control of storage

    Remove one download or select several to delete after confirmation. Watch history and viewing progress stay on your device.

Get started

Bring your
own provider.

You need an Xtream-compatible account and permission to access its content. IPTV Receiver does not include channels, media or a subscription.

  1. Install

    Download the APK and open it. Allow installation from your browser or file manager if Android asks.

  2. Connect

    Enter your provider’s server address, username and password. HTTP and HTTPS work automatically; prefer HTTPS when available.

  3. Watch

    Choose Live TV, Movies, Series or the TV guide. Your library comes from your provider.

A few useful answers

About IPTV Receiver

When will the screen turn off?

After playback pauses, stops, ends or fails, your device’s normal inactivity timeout applies. Brief buffering or reconnects keep the screen awake for up to one minute. Touching the screen resets Android’s inactivity timer; the app does not change your device settings.

Where is my viewing progress saved?

On this device, separately for each provider account. Resume points and watched status stay until you clear them. Uninstalling or clearing app storage removes local data; there is no cloud sync.

Where are offline downloads kept?

In private app storage by default, or in a local folder you choose in Settings → Downloads. Downloads stay until removed and are excluded from cache cleanup. Files in a shared folder can be opened by other apps and may remain after uninstall. Explicit logout hides the account’s library until you reconnect.

Can I use it on a TV or computer?

The current app is for Android phones and tablets. Dedicated Android TV, Windows, LG webOS and Samsung Tizen clients are planned.

Is it open source?

Yes. The project uses the MIT License, allowing use, modification and redistribution, including commercially. Keep the copyright and license notice. Provider content and third-party components have their own terms.
