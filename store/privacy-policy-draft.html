How the app works

IPTV Receiver is a player for a service you choose. It does not provide a subscription, channels or media. You enter your service's server address, username and password. These are sent to that service to authenticate and retrieve its catalog and streams.

Information used and recipients

Your provider receives authentication details and catalog/playback requests. Video, artwork and redirect hosts receive your device's network address and normal request information. Credential-bearing stream URLs may be sent to delivery hosts selected by your provider. Those services control their own retention and privacy practices. Review the privacy policy of each service you choose.

Local storage

If you select Remember connection, the app encrypts the connection details on your device using Android Keystore. Without that choice the connection remains in memory for the session. App backup is disabled. The app stores interface-language preferences and temporary artwork caches. It currently has no advertising, analytics or automatic crash-upload service and no developer-operated account backend.

Security and optional HTTP

Account and catalog connections require HTTPS. An optional Allow HTTP streams setting permits unencrypted media and artwork delivery for that connection, including redirects. HTTP traffic can be observed or modified on the network and may contain credential-bearing URLs. Keep this option off unless your provider requires it and you accept that exposure. No method of storage or transmission can guarantee absolute security.

Retention and removal

Sign out removes the remembered connection from this app. Android's Clear storage option removes local app data. Artwork caching is bounded and cleared periodically while the app is in the background; old files in the app's temporary directories are pruned during scheduled maintenance. Android may delay maintenance. The app does not retain durable viewing history. These actions do not delete your provider account or its logs; contact the provider for those requests.

Your choices and questions

You choose the provider, whether to remember the connection, whether to permit HTTP delivery, and the interface language. The app does not create provider accounts. For questions about this app, contact [SUPPORT EMAIL]. For provider-held information or service-account deletion, contact your provider.
