How the app works

IPTV Receiver is a player for a service you choose. It does not provide a subscription, channels or media. You enter your service's server address, username and password. These are sent to that service to authenticate and retrieve its catalog and streams.

Information used and recipients

Your provider receives authentication details and catalog/playback requests. Video, artwork and redirect hosts receive your device's network address and normal request information. Credential-bearing stream URLs may be sent to delivery hosts selected by your provider. Those services control their own retention and privacy practices. Review the privacy policy of each service you choose.

Local storage

If you select Remember connection, the app encrypts the connection details on your device using Android Keystore. Without that choice the connection remains in memory for the session. App backup is disabled. The app stores interface-language preferences, temporary artwork caches and separate viewing records for each provider account in an app-private database. History contains content/episode identifiers, display titles, stream extensions and playback-start timestamps. Movie/episode progress contains identifiers, playback positions, optional durations, completion/watched flags and last-played timestamps. These records contain no passwords, provider addresses or playback/artwork URLs and are not uploaded to a developer-operated service. The app also stores a salted parental-password verifier and category restrictions, plus account/expiry-specific acknowledgements for in-app subscription reminders. It currently has no advertising, analytics or automatic crash-upload service and no developer-operated account backend.

Security and HTTP support

Account, catalog, media and artwork connections support HTTP and HTTPS automatically. Media and artwork delivery can follow redirects between these protocols. HTTP traffic is unencrypted, can be observed or modified on the network and may contain account credentials or credential-bearing URLs. Prefer your provider's HTTPS address when available. HTTPS uses platform certificate verification. No method of storage or transmission can guarantee absolute security.

Retention and removal

Logout removes the remembered connection from this app and hides its viewing records. Movie and episode history uses a rolling 180-day window; live-channel history uses 10 days from the latest playback start. Watched status and resume points stay until explicitly cleared, independently of recent-history expiry. Logout and cache cleanup preserve these local records and parental settings. Expired history is removed on access or during scheduled maintenance; Android may delay maintenance. Settings → Watch history and progress → Clear for this account removes the connected account's history and progress after confirmation. There is no cloud sync or data export. Android Clear storage or uninstalling removes all local app records, preferences and parental settings. Artwork caching is bounded and cleared periodically while the app is in the background; old files in the app's temporary directories are pruned during scheduled maintenance. These actions do not delete your provider account or its logs; contact the provider for those requests.

Your choices and questions

You choose the provider, whether to remember the connection, the interface language and parental category restrictions. HTTP and HTTPS work automatically; choosing HTTP permits unencrypted requests. Adult blocking is enabled by default. A forgotten parental password cannot be recovered; resetting it removes the password and all accounts' custom category locks, restores adult blocking and requires new password setup. Subscription reminders appear only while browsing in the app; no system notifications are scheduled. The app does not create provider accounts. For questions about this app, contact [SUPPORT EMAIL]. For provider-held information or service-account deletion, contact your provider.
