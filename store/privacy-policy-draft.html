How the app works

IPTV Receiver is a player for a service you choose. It does not provide a subscription, channels or media. You enter your service's server address, username and password. These are sent to that service to authenticate and retrieve its catalog and streams.

Information used and recipients

Your provider receives authentication details and catalog/playback requests. Video, artwork and redirect hosts receive your device's network address and normal request information. Credential-bearing stream URLs may be sent to delivery hosts selected by your provider. Those services control their own retention and privacy practices. Review the privacy policy of each service you choose.

Local storage

If you select Remember connection, the app encrypts the connection details on your device using Android Keystore. Without that choice the connection remains in memory for the session. App backup is disabled. The app stores interface-language preferences, temporary artwork caches and a separate watch history for each provider account. History contains the identifiers, titles and last-watch times of live channels, movies and episodes, plus the format information needed to replay them. History does not contain your password or playback/artwork URLs and is not uploaded to a developer-operated service. It currently has no advertising, analytics or automatic crash-upload service and no developer-operated account backend.

Security and HTTP support

Account, catalog, media and artwork connections support HTTP and HTTPS automatically. Media and artwork delivery can follow redirects between these protocols. HTTP traffic is unencrypted, can be observed or modified on the network and may contain account credentials or credential-bearing URLs. Prefer your provider's HTTPS address when available. HTTPS uses platform certificate verification. No method of storage or transmission can guarantee absolute security.

Retention and removal

Sign out removes the remembered connection from this app and hides its watch history. History uses a rolling 10-day window from each stream's latest viewing and is preserved when you sign out, clear cache or remove temporary files. Expired entries are removed when history is accessed or during scheduled maintenance; Android may delay maintenance. Android's Clear storage option removes local app data, including all watch history. Artwork caching is bounded and cleared periodically while the app is in the background; old files in the app's temporary directories are pruned during scheduled maintenance. These actions do not delete your provider account or its logs; contact the provider for those requests.

Your choices and questions

You choose the provider, whether to remember the connection, whether to permit HTTP delivery, and the interface language. The app does not create provider accounts.
